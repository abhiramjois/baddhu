<h1 align="center">Baddhu</h1>

<p align="center">A tiny Android app that opens your Instagram messages and gets out of the way.</p>

---

## Why this exists

Instagram used to be a phone book.

Then it became a casino.

Somewhere along the way, the app you opened to reply to a friend grew an Explore tab, a Reels tab, a Shop, a Suggested-for-you row, and roughly four hundred ways for a stranger on the other side of the planet to end up on your screen. The people you actually talk to got moved down to a tab called **Messages**, like they were an afterthought in their own app.

Baddhu is a small Android shell that opens **only** your messages.

- **Explore is gone.** Not hidden in a menu — the routes don't resolve. There's no button to resist, because there's no button.
- **The Reels tab is gone.** You can still watch a reel a friend actually sent you, in full, in the shape it was made in.
- **Shopping is gone.** So is Create, and the "accounts to follow" row that only ever suggests people you already follow.
- **The ad tracking is gone.** Google's ad networks and Instagram's ad logging are blocked at the request level, so nothing here is building a profile of you to sell.
- **Their branding is gone.** Your wordmark sits where their logo used to be.

What's left is the rarest thing on the internet: a messaging app with nothing to do but message people.

To be clear, Baddhu is not going to make you a better person. It has no streaks, no badges, no daily streak to protect, nothing to check in on. It just removes the slot machine and leaves the phone book.

It's the smallest possible rebellion against an app that decided your messages were the least interesting part of it.

## What you get

- **Messages only.** Navigation is restricted to an allow-list of DM routes. Everything else goes home.
- **Your branding.** A wordmark and tagline in place of Instagram's chrome.
- **Reels your friends send you,** playing properly instead of getting squashed into a box.
- **A real system navigation bar,** correctly inset, the way Android is supposed to look.
- **No backend, no account handling, no analytics, no ads.** You sign in with your own Instagram account and the app talks to instagram.com directly.

## Install

Grab the APK from the [releases page](https://github.com/abhiramjois/baddhu/releases) and open it, allowing installs from your file manager when prompted. Android 8.0 or newer.

## A note on the name

Baddhu is Kannada for "good friend" — which felt like the right word for an app whose entire ambition is to let you get back to the people you actually like.
